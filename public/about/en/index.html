Isshoikka
Ikebana · One Life, One Flower
Zen and flower, one taste: in the nature of a flower, we meet the nature of life.

Isshoikka began in 2015 to bring Zen into the practice of ikebana — the flower as a mirror in which we see ourselves. It now has 260K followers, and its workshops in Silicon Valley are warmly received.

Its founder, Xuan Guang, has practiced ikebana for more than a decade, studying and exhibiting many times at the Ikenobo headquarters in Kyoto, and holds the Ikenobo teaching license. Zen practice flows into every arrangement, opening a deep and quiet joy of being one with nature. Carried into daily life, this same “Zen and flower, one taste” becomes more ease, more coherence, more naturalness.

Silence
Workshop · Isshoikka
No phones. No talking.
No assumptions. No judgement.

Pick up the flower. Look. Cut. Place. Every moment is awareness; every stillness, a quiet joy.

“In a space so quiet and so fluid, I felt the beauty of plants as they are. With every cut of a branch or leaf, I was feeling the plant itself. Arranging the material to follow the form of ikebana became a journey inward, asking what is natural and what is beautiful.” — Student C.

One Stem, the Whole World
Ikebana · Three Ways of Seeing

Impermanence · “Standing before the wind that scatters blossoms and strips the leaves, one may receive the seed of awakening.”

Nature · “Let yourself listen to the silent, hidden order of nature.”

Oneness · “The natural form is the root of expression; within it lies the ideal image of what we perceive.”

“Observing and trimming with full attention, I found that a single material could show such different character. Along the way it opened many reflections on philosophy and on life.” — Student K.

Worldly ikebana seeks the pleasure of the senses;
transcendent ikebana seeks the resonance of feeling;
ultimate ikebana is the oneness of human and nature.
The nature of the flower is our own nature.
01 · Isshoikka · isshoikka.com
Xuan Guang
Li Qian

Xuan Guang, born Li Qian, has family roots in Hangzhou and lives in Silicon Valley. Bachelor’s degrees from Zhejiang University’s Chu Kochen Honors College and the University of Hong Kong; master’s and PhD in computer science from the University of Michigan. More than ten years at Facebook and Google as an engineering manager.

Raised in a family steeped in the arts, with interests spanning poetry, music, calligraphy, seal carving, ikebana, tea, kendo, ceramics, woodwork, origami, hanfu and collecting. Founder of Isshoikka, the Taixuan Guqin Society, the Jiuge Poetry Society, Ocean & Morus, Starlia, Lumilab.AI and the Inner Exploration Lab.

Many years of sustained Zen practice and volunteer work, devoted to bringing inner exploration into every field — to inspire a life that is complete, perfect, joyful and happy.
